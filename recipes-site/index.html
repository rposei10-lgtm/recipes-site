Chocolate Fudge

Description

Chocolate fudge is a classic confection that combines rich cocoa with a smooth, creamy texture. This indulgent treat is perfect for any occasion and can be made right in your own kitchen.

The key to perfect fudge is careful temperature control and proper mixing techniques. Follow these steps to create delicious homemade fudge that stays fresh for weeks.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups granulated sugar
  • 3/4 cup unsweetened cocoa powder
  • 1 cup milk
  • 1/2 cup butter
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
  • Pinch of salt

Steps

  1. Combine sugar, cocoa powder, milk, and butter in a saucepan. Heat over medium, stirring constantly until it reaches 238°F (soft ball stage).
  2. Remove from heat and let cool for 5 minutes. Add vanilla and salt, then beat until thick. Pour into a buttered pan and cool before cutting.
